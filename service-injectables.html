Neuromodulator

Jeuveau®

$12 per unit

Dubbed "Newtox," Jeuveau® is a modern, FDA-approved alternative to Botox with the same protein family and equally impressive results. Many clients find it works faster with a smoother onset.

  • Great option for Botox-resistant clients

Most clients enjoy Botox and Jeuveau results for 3–4 months. First-time clients may metabolize it a bit faster; with regular treatments spaced at the right interval, many clients find results lasting closer to 4–5 months. We recommend booking your follow-up appointment before results fully wear off to maintain a consistently smooth look.

Lip filler discomfort is very manageable. The hyaluronic acid fillers used at Nurture & Glow contain built-in lidocaine to numb as they're injected. We also apply a topical numbing cream prior to treatment. Most clients describe the sensation as mild pressure rather than pain — and the appointment is typically complete in under 30 minutes.
