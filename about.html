Our Story

Built From Heart

A Nurse Practitioner. A mother of three. A dream brought to life — for women who deserve to feel radiant.

Licensed Nurse Practitioner
The Founder

Meet Audrey Porras

For years, I worked as a Nurse Practitioner — caring for patients, managing complex cases, and pouring myself into the health of others. I loved it. But I had a vision.

I dreamed of a place where women could come not just to be treated, but to be truly seen. Where clinical expertise met warmth. Where safety and luxury coexisted. Where every woman left feeling more like herself — radiant, confident, and cared for.

As a single mother raising three children, I know firsthand how easy it is to put yourself last. I built Nurture & Glow as a reminder — you deserve to be nurtured too. Proudly serving Arlington, Fort Worth, Grand Prairie, and the greater DFW metroplex.

"You don't have to choose between safe and beautiful. At Nurture & Glow, you get both."
