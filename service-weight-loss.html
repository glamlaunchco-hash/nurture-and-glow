What are Semaglutide & Tirzepatide?

Semaglutide and Tirzepatide are FDA-approved GLP-1 receptor agonist medications that have transformed the landscape of medical weight loss. Originally developed to manage blood sugar in Type 2 diabetes, these medications have demonstrated remarkable efficacy for sustainable, clinically meaningful weight reduction.

Unlike fad diets or stimulant-based supplements, GLP-1 medications work by mimicking natural hormones your body already produces — reducing hunger signals, slowing gastric emptying, and improving insulin sensitivity. The result is a physiological shift that makes eating less feel natural, not forced.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Semaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist — it mimics a single gut hormone to reduce appetite and improve blood sugar control. Tirzepatide is a dual GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonist, meaning it activates two hormone pathways simultaneously. Clinical trials have shown Tirzepatide produces greater average weight loss than Semaglutide, though individual results vary. Both are effective and proven — your provider will help you choose based on your health profile and goals.

Clinical studies show average weight loss of 10–15% of body weight with Semaglutide and 15–22% with Tirzepatide over approximately 72–84 weeks when combined with lifestyle modifications. Individual results vary based on starting weight, adherence, diet, and activity level. Most patients begin noticing reduced appetite within the first 2–4 weeks and meaningful weight loss within 8–12 weeks.

The most common side effects are gastrointestinal — nausea, mild stomach discomfort, or changes in bowel habits — and they are most pronounced early in the program. These typically subside as your body adjusts. Our gradual dose escalation schedule is specifically designed to minimize side effects. Your NP will discuss the full side effect profile and what to watch for during your consultation.
