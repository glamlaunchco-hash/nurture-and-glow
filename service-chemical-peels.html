What Is the VI Peel?

A Peel That Works for Every Skin Type

The VI Peel is a medium-depth chemical peel formulated with a synergistic blend of trichloroacetic acid (TCA), retinoic acid, salicylic acid, phenol, and vitamin C. Unlike many chemical peels that are only appropriate for light skin tones, the VI Peel was specifically designed to be safe and effective for all Fitzpatrick skin types — from the fairest complexions to deeper melanin-rich skin.

Applied in a single in-office session, the VI Peel penetrates beyond the superficial epidermis to stimulate cell turnover, break up pigmented cells, and promote the growth of fresh, healthy skin beneath. The peeling process begins 2–3 days after your appointment and completes within 7 days — revealing remarkably clearer, smoother, more luminous skin underneath.

At Nurture & Glow in Arlington, TX, VI Peels are administered by Audrey Porras, NP, who will select the appropriate VI Peel formulation for your specific skin concern — whether that's hyperpigmentation, acne, fine lines, or overall renewal. Each session is $250 with no hidden fees.

What to Expect

Downtime & Aftercare

The VI Peel is a medium-depth peel — meaning it delivers more significant results than a light superficial peel, with a proportionally more involved recovery period. Planning ahead makes the process smooth and comfortable.

Day 1–2: Skin may feel tight and look slightly pink or bronzed after the peel is applied. You'll leave the office with the peel solution on — it is washed off at home 4–6 hours later using the provided post-peel towelettes. Mild discomfort or a warm sensation is normal.

Days 2–3: Peeling begins. The skin will start to flake and shed, beginning around the mouth, chin, and forehead. This is the process working — do not pick, peel, or pull at the skin. Keep your skincare simple: gentle cleanser, provided post-peel moisturizer, and broad-spectrum SPF 50+ daily.

Days 4–7: Peeling continues and then completes. By day 7, the shedding is finished and your new skin is revealed. Most clients describe this moment as transformative — skin looks dramatically clearer, smoother, and more luminous.

After Day 7: Results continue to improve for 2–4 weeks as cell turnover completes. Maintain results with daily SPF and the recommended post-peel skincare routine Audrey will provide.

Chemical Peel FAQ

Everything you need to know before booking your VI Peel at Nurture & Glow in Arlington, TX.

The VI Peel application involves a tingling or mild stinging sensation as the solution is applied — this is brief and subsides quickly. The VI Peel formula contains a small amount of phenol, which actually has a self-anesthetizing quality, making the treatment more comfortable than many other medium-depth peels. After the application, most clients feel comfortable enough to drive home and go about their day (until washing off the peel 4–6 hours later). The peeling phase itself is not painful — it feels like dry, tight skin similar to a mild sunburn.

Many clients see a meaningful improvement after just one VI Peel session. For general skin renewal and brightness, a single session may be sufficient with periodic maintenance every 3–4 months. For more stubborn concerns like melasma, deep hyperpigmentation, or significant acne scarring, a series of 3–4 peels spaced 4–6 weeks apart typically delivers the most comprehensive results. Audrey will give you a personalized recommendation based on your skin assessment and your goals during your consultation.

Yes — the VI Peel was specifically formulated to be safe for all Fitzpatrick skin types, including Types IV, V, and VI (medium-dark to very dark skin tones). This is one of the most significant advantages of the VI Peel over other chemical peel systems. The Precision Plus formula is particularly well-suited for treating hyperpigmentation in deeper skin tones, where other aggressive treatments can trigger post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH) and make pigmentation worse. Audrey's clinical background ensures she selects the safest, most appropriate protocol for your skin tone.

VI Peels pair beautifully with other Nurture & Glow treatments in a strategic sequence. A common approach is to alternate microneedling and VI Peel sessions 4–6 weeks apart — together they address different layers of the skin and deliver synergistic results that neither can achieve alone. Adding IV Glow Drip therapy in the days surrounding your peel supports skin healing and enhances results from the inside. We do not recommend combining a VI Peel with Botox or filler on the same day, but these can be done 2–4 weeks before or after. Audrey will help design a complementary treatment calendar during your consultation.
