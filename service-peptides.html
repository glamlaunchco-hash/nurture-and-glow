What Are Peptides?

Peptides are short chains of amino acids — the building blocks of proteins — that act as biological messengers in the body. They signal cells to perform specific functions: repair tissue, stimulate growth hormone release, reduce inflammation, support fat metabolism, or enhance immune function.

Unlike hormones, peptides work in targeted, precise ways with a favorable safety profile. They are naturally occurring molecules that your body already recognizes and uses. As we age, production of key peptides declines — therapeutic supplementation helps restore and optimize these signaling pathways.

At Nurture & Glow, peptide therapy is individualized. Audrey reviews your goals and health history to create a protocol specifically matched to your needs — not a one-size-fits-all approach.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Peptides are naturally occurring molecules and have a strong safety profile when used appropriately under clinical supervision. Some peptides (like certain growth hormone fragments) are regulated, which is why they must be prescribed by a licensed provider. At Nurture & Glow, all peptide protocols are prescribed and monitored by Audrey Porras, a licensed NP, and sourced from reputable compounding pharmacies. We never recommend or prescribe anything outside a clinical context.

Administration method depends on the peptide. Most therapeutic peptides — including CJC-1295/Ipamorelin, BPC-157, and TB-500 — are administered via subcutaneous injection (small needle just under the skin). The needles are very fine and most patients find them comfortable. Some peptides can also be taken orally or intranasally. We provide thorough training and you'll feel confident before you ever administer a dose at home.

Results timeline varies by peptide and goal. BPC-157 for injury recovery can produce noticeable effects within 2–4 weeks. CJC-1295/Ipamorelin for growth hormone optimization typically shows meaningful benefits at 6–12 weeks with consistent use. Sleep and energy improvements are often noticed within the first few weeks. Peptide therapy is generally a longer-term strategy — many protocols run for 3–6+ month cycles for maximum benefit.
