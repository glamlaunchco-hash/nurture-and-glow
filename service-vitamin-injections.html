Skip the Pill. Get Results.

Oral vitamins and supplements must survive your digestive system before any of the active nutrients reach your bloodstream. The reality is that oral absorption rates for many key nutrients — particularly B12 and fat-soluble vitamins like Vitamin D — can be significantly compromised by gut health, diet, age, and medication interactions.

Intramuscular vitamin injections deliver nutrients directly into the muscle, where they absorb into the bloodstream at near 100% bioavailability. The effect is faster, more reliable, and more efficient than even the highest-quality oral supplement.

At Nurture & Glow, all injections are administered by Audrey Porras, a licensed Nurse Practitioner. A quick 5-minute visit — and you feel the difference.

Who Benefits Most from Vitamin Injections?

Vitamin injections are a great fit for a wide range of people. If any of the following apply to you, it may be time to book a shot.

You Might Need B12 If You...

  • Follow a vegetarian or vegan diet

You Might Need Vitamin D If You...
