How It Works

Your Skin's Own Healing Power

Microneedling — also called Collagen Induction Therapy (CIT) — uses a medical-grade device to create thousands of precise, controlled micro-channels in the skin's surface. These micro-injuries trigger your body's natural wound-healing cascade, flooding the treated area with growth factors and stimulating the production of new collagen and elastin.

The result over the following weeks and months is visibly smoother, firmer, more even-toned skin. Scar tissue is gradually replaced with healthy new skin cells. Pores visibly shrink. Fine lines soften. Hyperpigmentation fades. And none of it requires harsh chemicals, laser energy, or prolonged downtime.

At Nurture & Glow in Arlington, TX, we use medical-grade microneedling devices — not the consumer-grade derma rollers you'll find at home — delivering consistent needle depth across every millimeter of your skin for safe, predictable, and impressive results.

What to Expect

Your Treatment Journey

Microneedling results are progressive, not instantaneous — here's what your skin will look like at each stage of the process.

1

Day of Your Session

Topical numbing cream is applied 30–40 minutes before treatment. The microneedling pass takes 20–30 minutes, followed by a calming serum. Your skin will look and feel similar to a mild sunburn immediately after — this is completely normal and expected. Plan to go home and rest; avoid makeup for 24 hours.

2

Days 1–3: Initial Healing

Redness and mild sensitivity are at their peak during the first 24–48 hours, then begin to fade rapidly. Your skin may feel dry or slightly tight — this is your collagen cascade activating. Stay out of the sun, keep your skincare simple (gentle cleanser, moisturizer, SPF), and avoid active ingredients like retinol and acids for one week.

3

Weeks 2–4: Visible Improvement

As new collagen fibers form and mature, you'll start to notice your skin looking smoother and more radiant. Pores appear smaller. Early improvements in texture and mild scarring become visible. Many clients see a glow they haven't had in years. This is the stage where most people start getting compliments.

4

Sessions 2–3 and Beyond: Optimal Results

The most significant, lasting improvements come from completing a series of 3–6 sessions. Acne scars continue to fill in. Firmness noticeably improves. Hyperpigmentation lightens. After completing your series, most clients maintain results with 1–2 sessions per year. This is when the transformation becomes truly remarkable.

Common Questions

Microneedling FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most often about microneedling at Nurture & Glow in Arlington, TX.

Most clients are pleasantly surprised by how comfortable microneedling is with proper preparation. We apply a clinical-strength topical numbing cream 30–40 minutes before beginning, which significantly reduces sensation. During treatment, you'll feel a mild buzzing or scratching pressure — uncomfortable for some in sensitive areas like the forehead or around the nose, but generally very tolerable. The treatment itself takes under 30 minutes. If you have specific comfort concerns, let us know and we'll accommodate you.

Yes — microneedling is one of the few aesthetic treatments that is safe and effective across all Fitzpatrick skin types, including deeper skin tones (Types IV–VI) that are often excluded from laser resurfacing treatments. Because microneedling works mechanically rather than with light or heat energy, there is no risk of the hyperpigmentation that lasers can cause in melanin-rich skin. It's one of the reasons microneedling has become the preferred skin resurfacing option for women of color.

We recommend waiting a minimum of 24 hours before applying any makeup after your microneedling session. The micro-channels in the skin are open during this window — applying makeup prematurely can introduce bacteria and cause breakouts or irritation. After 24 hours, mineral-based makeup is safest. Full makeup can typically be resumed at 48 hours. Plan your treatment around a low-key day or weekend if possible.
