This Is Personal

"Motherhood changed everything I thought I knew about strength, vulnerability, and the kind of care that actually matters."

Maternal wellness is not just another service offering at Nurture & Glow — it is the soul of why this practice exists. As a licensed Nurse Practitioner and a mother of three children, Audrey Porras brings something that no certification alone can provide: lived experience.

She knows what it is to navigate the uncertainty of a birth plan, the physical and emotional weight of those early postpartum weeks, and the deeply personal struggle of establishing a nursing relationship with a newborn. She has sat with that 2 a.m. worry, and she has emerged with both clinical expertise and genuine empathy.

When you come to Audrey for maternal wellness support, you are not a patient number. You are a mother — and she meets you exactly where you are.

"My goal is to be the provider I wish I'd had — someone who combines real clinical knowledge with the understanding that only comes from having been there."

— Audrey Porras, NP · Mother of Three

Service One

Childbirth Preparation

Knowledge is power — and peace. Audrey works with you to create a birth experience that is informed, empowered, and uniquely yours.

Birth Planning

We work through your birth preferences together — hospital vs. birth center, pain management options, labor support, and how to communicate your wishes effectively to your care team. Your birth plan is a living document; we help you make it reflect you.

  • Personalized birth plan development
  • Understanding labor stages and what to expect
  • Medication and natural pain management options
  • Advocacy skills for hospital settings
  • Partner and support person preparation

Prenatal Wellness

Your physical and emotional wellbeing during pregnancy shapes the foundation for birth and the postpartum period. We address holistic prenatal wellness including nutrition, hydration, safe supplementation, and stress management — from a clinical perspective.

  • Prenatal nutrition guidance
  • Safe supplementation and hydration support
  • IV hydration for first trimester nausea
  • Energy and fatigue management
  • Stress, anxiety, and emotional preparation
Service Two

Lactation Support

Breastfeeding is natural — but it is not always easy. Audrey provides clinical and experiential guidance to help you establish and sustain a nursing relationship that works for you and your baby.

You Do Not Have to Figure This Out Alone

The early days of breastfeeding can be among the most challenging experiences of new motherhood. Latch issues, milk supply concerns, engorgement, nipple pain, and the emotional weight of wanting to succeed — these are real, common, and addressable with the right support.

Audrey's approach is never judgmental and never one-size-fits-all. She meets you where you are — whether you want to breastfeed exclusively, supplement, or simply get enough information to make an informed choice that feels right for your family.

Latch assessment and correction techniques
Milk supply evaluation and support strategies
Engorgement and mastitis guidance
Nipple pain and wound management
Pumping strategy and milk storage
Returning to work and maintaining supply
Weaning support when the time is right

Your Consultation

What to Expect When You Book

No rushing, no scripts, no judgment. Your consultation is a real conversation designed around you.

1

Intake & Goals

We begin with your story — where you are in your pregnancy or postpartum journey, what support you're looking for, and what has or hasn't worked before. This is a conversation, not a questionnaire.

2

Clinical Assessment

Audrey reviews any relevant health history, current symptoms, medications, and prenatal or postpartum concerns. For lactation visits, this includes a feeding assessment — we observe a feeding session if possible.

3

Personalized Plan

You leave with a clear, practical plan — whether that's a birth preparation roadmap, specific breastfeeding techniques to try, nutritional guidance, or referrals to complementary care providers when appropriate.

4

Ongoing Support

Motherhood is not a single appointment. Follow-up visits are available as your needs evolve — whether pre-delivery, immediately postpartum, or weeks into your nursing journey. We are here for the whole arc of it.

Pricing for maternal wellness consultations is provided individually. Contact us to discuss your needs and schedule.
