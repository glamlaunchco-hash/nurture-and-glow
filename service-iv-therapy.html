What Is IV Therapy?

Nutrients That Actually Reach Your Cells

When you take oral vitamins, your digestive system absorbs only a fraction of what you swallow — often as little as 20–30% — before nutrients are broken down and excreted. IV therapy bypasses the gut entirely, delivering vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants directly into your bloodstream at 100% bioavailability.

The result? You feel it — fast. Most clients notice increased energy, improved mental clarity, and a visible skin glow within hours of their first session. Whether you're recovering from a late night, fighting off a cold, prepping for a big event, or simply investing in your long-term wellness, there's a drip designed for exactly where you are right now.

At Nurture & Glow in Arlington, TX, every IV therapy session is supervised by Audrey Porras, NP, ensuring medical safety throughout your infusion. We use only pharmaceutical-grade ingredients, and every drip is formulated to clinical standards.
